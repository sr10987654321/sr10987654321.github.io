Sentence pattern

Five short moves. One paragraph.

1Hear something
2See an unclear shape
3Notice one clue
4Show a body reaction
5Ask a suspenseful question

Keep this sequence. Change the details to match our harbour picture.

THE MYSTERIOUS FIGURE · DAY 2 — BUILD-UP 09 / 16
We do · sentence 4

Show fear. Do not tell it.

Oliver’s hands trembled as he watched.

We could say “Oliver was scared.” But that tells us.

His breath caught in his throat · His knees shook · His stomach tightened

Choose a reaction that fits your character. Say it, then write it.

THE MYSTERIOUS FIGURE · DAY 2 — BUILD-UP 14 / 16
Teacher reference · reveal only after writing

One POSSIBLE shared build-up.

At that moment, Oliver heard something scraping along the cobblestones, hidden by the shadows. A dark figure slipped behind a streetlight. For a moment, the outline of a tall hat appeared beneath the dim yellow glow. Oliver’s hands trembled as he watched. Who was standing there?

This is the teacher model from our five-day guide. The class version can — and should — use students’ choices.
