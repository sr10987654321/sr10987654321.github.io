The HASS connection

An Australian tradition

Australians played Two-up during the nineteenth century. Australian soldiers later made it strongly associated with military life and Anzac Day.

Historical thinking

Games can reveal how people spent their free time, built friendships and carried traditions with them.

Today we investigate the history and mathematics. We use classroom points, not money.

Are heads or tails now “due”?

No. The next toss still has the same four possible combinations.

Previous results describe what happened. They do not control the next toss.

What we can predict

Odds should occur more often over many tosses because it has two possible combinations.

What we cannot predict

We cannot know the exact result of the next toss.
