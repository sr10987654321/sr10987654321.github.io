TRAINING: THE SUPPLY CONVEYOR

There are 6 trays. Each tray carries 14 glue sticks. How many glue sticks are there altogether?

How could we partition 14 to make this easier?

6 × 14 = (6 × 10) + (6 × 4) = 60 + 24 = 84 glue sticks.
Mission 01 / 05 • MULTIPLY

STICKER OVERLOAD

Evil Ducky loads 7 trays with 18 stickers on each tray. How many stickers are there altogether?

Solve it on your mission sheet. Explain your method.

7 × 18 = 7 × (10 + 8) = 70 + 56 = 126. Code digit: 6 (ones digit).

Mission 02 / 05 • DIVIDE

PENCIL PARCELS

The machine sorts 168 pencils equally into 7 locked parcels. How many pencils are in each parcel?

Solve it on your mission sheet. Explain your method.

168 ÷ 7 = 24 because 7 × 24 = 168. Code digit: 4 (ones digit).

Mission 03 / 05 • MULTIPLY

CHAIR CLONER

Nine rows of 16 chairs appear in the hall. How many chairs has the machine produced?

Solve it on your mission sheet. Explain your method.

9 × 16 = (9 × 10) + (9 × 6) = 90 + 54 = 144. Code digit: 4 (ones digit).

Mission 04 / 05 • DIVIDE

THE TICKET SPLITTER

Evil Ducky has 216 tickets and divides them equally among 8 boxes. How many tickets go in each box?

Solve it on your mission sheet. Explain your method.

216 ÷ 8 = 27 because 8 × 27 = 216. Code digit: 7 (ones digit).

Mission 05 / 05 • TWO-STEP

FINAL SUPPLY DROP

There are 12 crates with 15 markers in each. The markers are shared equally between 9 classrooms. How many markers does each classroom receive?

Solve it on your mission sheet. Explain your method.

12 × 15 = 180; 180 ÷ 9 = 20. Code digit: 0 (ones digit).
