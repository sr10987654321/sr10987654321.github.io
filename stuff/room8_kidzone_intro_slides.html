MISSION 01MULTIPLICATION + SUBTRACTION

PENCIL PANIC!

8 boxes. 24 pencils in each box. 137 pencils handed out.

How many are left?

Write the TENS digit of the answer.

8 × 24 = 192; 192 − 137 = 55.
Code digit: 5
MISSION 02GEOMETRY

STICKY SQUARES!

You have 24 matchsticks. Each square uses 4. No shared sides or sticks.

How many separate squares?

24 ÷ 4 = 6 squares.
Code digit: 6
MISSION 03ELAPSED TIME

TIME IS TICKING!

A movie begins at 10:35 am and finishes at 12:10 pm.

How many minutes?

Keep adding the digits until one remains.

10:35 → 11:35 = 60 min; → 12:10 = 35 min.
95 → 9 + 5 = 14 → 1 + 4 = 5.
MISSION 04MONEY + MULTI-STEP PROBLEMS

LUNCH MONEY
MAYHEM!

Three students each buy a $4.50 lunch and a $2 drink. They pay with one $20 note.

How much change?

How many WHOLE dollars are in the change?

3 × ($4.50 + $2) = $19.50.
Change = 50c. Whole dollars = 0.
MISSION 05NUMBER LOGIC

ODD ONE OUT!

I am odd. Greater than 3 but less than 9. Four times my value is greater than 20 but less than 30.

What number am I?

?
Odd options: 5 or 7.
5 × 4 = 20 (not greater). 7 × 4 = 28.
Code digit: 7.
