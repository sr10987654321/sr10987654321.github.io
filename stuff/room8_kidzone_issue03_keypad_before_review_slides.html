TRAINING: MR RICHMONDSON'S JOKE LEAFLETS

Mr Richmondson printed 8 bundles of 17 joke leaflets. He gave 29 leaflets away. How many leaflets are left?

What should we calculate first? Can we partition 17?

8 × 17 = (8 × 10) + (8 × 7) = 80 + 56 = 136. Then 136 − 29 = 107 leaflets.

Mission 01 / 05 • MULTIPLY

THE APOLOGY POSTERS

Mr Richmondson printed 16 bundles of 24 apology posters. They also found 9 boxes containing 18 old posters each. How many posters do they have altogether?

Show your strategy. Take the ones digit of your answer.

16 × 24 = 384; 9 × 18 = 162; 384 + 162 = 546. Ones digit: 6.

Mission 02 / 05 • DIVIDE

DUCKIE GOODIE BAGS

Mr Richmondson has 672 little thank-you stickers to share equally between 8 Duckies. Each Duckie uses 27 stickers to decorate an apology card. How many stickers does each Duckie have left?

Show your strategy. Take the ones digit of your answer.

672 ÷ 8 = 84; 84 − 27 = 57. Check: 8 × 84 = 672. Ones digit: 7.
CASE FILE 02

ONES DIGIT = CODE DIGIT 2

Mission 03 / 05 • MULTIPLY

THE CUPBOARD CLEAN-UP

There are 18 shelves in the big cupboard, with 26 items on each shelf. The Duckies remove 39 broken items. How many items are still on the shelves?

Show your strategy. Take the ones digit of your answer.

18 × 26 = 468; 468 − 39 = 429. Ones digit: 9.
CASE FILE 03

ONES DIGIT = CODE DIGIT 3

Mission 04 / 05 • DIVIDE

THE JOKE BOOK SORT-OUT

Mr Richmondson collected 936 joke cards. They sort them equally into 9 storage tubs. Then 28 cards are taken out of each tub because they are repeated jokes. How many different cards remain in each tub?

Show your strategy. Take the ones digit of your answer.

936 ÷ 9 = 104; 104 − 28 = 76. Check: 9 × 104 = 936. Ones digit: 6.
CASE FILE 04

ONES DIGIT = CODE DIGIT 4

Mission 05 / 05 • TWO-STEP

THE APOLOGY AFTERNOON TEA

Mr Richmondson orders 24 boxes of mini biscuits, with 18 biscuits in each box. He shares them equally between 8 tables. The Duckies eat 19 biscuits from each table before anyone arrives. How many biscuits are left on each table?

Show your strategy. Take the ones digit of your answer.

24 × 18 = 432; 432 ÷ 8 = 54; 54 − 19 = 35. Ones digit: 5.
