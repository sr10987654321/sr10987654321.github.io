Today's writing challenge
Make five sentences come alive.
Take five plain sentences. Transform each one. Then connect them into one suspenseful paragraph.
5 sentences1 paragraphYour imagination
The big idea
Don't just tell us what happened. Make us see it.

Plain

The cat walked down the road.

Alive

The scruffy black cat crept along the deserted road, its yellow eyes searching the darkness.

What changed? Talk to your partner.
Our writer's toolkit
Three ways to bring words to life.

1 · Powerful verbs

walked → crept, prowled, slunk

2 · Precise details

the road → the deserted, moonlit road

3 · Show, don't tell

scared → its ears flattened and its tail stiffened

Bonus · Personification

the wind whispered through the trees

Your mission
Write each improved sentence in your book.

Keep the main event the same, but make it vivid. Aim for one strong sentence, not a whole paragraph.

After all five, we'll join them into one piece of writing.

Final writing challenge · Combine

The big reveal · Before and after
Same events. Different impact.

Original — plain

The black cat walked down the road. The wind blew through the trees. The cat heard a noise. Something moved in the shadows. The cat ran away.

Behind a rusted bin, a long, crooked shape shifted silently in the shadows. With a startled yowl, the cat bolted around the corner and vanished into the darkness.
