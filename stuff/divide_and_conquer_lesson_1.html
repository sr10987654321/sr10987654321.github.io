Teacher controls

Lesson flow: Hook → prediction → concrete equal sharing → introduce notation → pair investigation → test groups → factor patterns → inverse operations → 25 extension → exit ticket.

Materials: 24 counters per pair, cups or paper circles, whiteboards and maths books.

Teacher move: Require students to say what the quotient means in the context, not just state a number. Use group size 1 or 24 to discuss boundary cases.
