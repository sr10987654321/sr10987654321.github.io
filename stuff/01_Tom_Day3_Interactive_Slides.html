The problem

The character hides.
The threat moves.
The tension rises.

No escape yet — that’s tomorrow!
Teacher cue: Recall Day 1 opening and Day 2 build-up. No need to reteach everything.
Goal

What are we learning?

We are learning to build suspense as our character hides from danger.

  • Choose a precise hiding place.
  • Use a powerful movement verb.
  • Show fear through a physical reaction.
  • Keep the figure mysterious.
Teacher cue: Read the LI. Point to criteria; say that students will check these at the end.
Retrieve

Where did we leave Tom?

A dark figure slipped behind a streetlight. For a moment, the outline of a tall hat appeared beneath the dim yellow glow. Tom's hands trembled. Who was standing there?

What does Tom know? What does he not know?
He has seen a dark figure and the outline of a tall hat, but does not know who it is. His hands are already trembling.

Connect to the model

What did Zelda do?

“Without thinking, Zelda dashed under a lorry and crouched as still as stone.”
Which part tells us where she hides?
“under a lorry” is the hiding place.
Teacher cue: Read the line aloud, then ask students to gesture hide/freeze.
I DO

Why not “walked”?

The figure walked along the harbour.
The figure stalked along the harbour.
Which word makes the figure seem more dangerous? Why?
“Stalked” suggests deliberate, threatening movement. It creates suspense.
Teacher cue: Say why a word was chosen, not just that it is “better”.
