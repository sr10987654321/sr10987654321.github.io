The answer

Heads and tails are still equally likely

Heads

1 out of 2

Tails

1 out of 2

READ ALOUDThe coin has not changed. It does not remember the earlier results. Every fair toss starts again with the same two possible outcomes.

Pair investigation

How common are streaks?

Work with a partner and one coin.

Toss it 20 times. Record every result in order using H or T.

Circle any streak of three or more matching results.

Write down your longest streak.

Challenge

Six heads appear in a row

H H H H H H

Does this prove the coin is unfair?

No. A long streak can happen by chance.

It gives us a reason to investigate further, but it does not prove the coin is unfair.
