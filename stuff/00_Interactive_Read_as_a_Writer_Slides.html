ZELDA CLAW · READ AS A WRITER 02
Toolkit recap

Four ways to build suspense

1. Physical reactions

Zelda shivered / Zelda’s fur prickled

2. Rhetorical questions

What was it? / Had it seen her?

3. Movement verbs

slipped / paced / slinking

4. Hiding the threat

something / A vague shape / A green eye

ZELDA CLAW · READ AS A WRITER 11
Shared innovation

Read the complete model

All night, rain swept across the empty harbour. A child stood beside an old stone wall, watching the darkness. A streetlamp flickered, and footsteps echoed along the deserted path. The child shivered. Where could they hide?

At that moment, a faint scraping noise came from behind the harbour steps. A shadowy figure slipped beneath a streetlamp. The child’s hands trembled as they watched. Who was standing there?

Without thinking, the child ducked behind a wooden boat and crouched as still as stone. Silently, the tall man paced through the darkness, stalking

along the wet stones. Had he noticed the child?Slowly, the stranger drew closer and closer. His shoes scraped against the pavement as the lamplight stretched his shadow. The child could bear it no longer. They darted up the steps and raced towards the houses. The footsteps did not follow. They were safe — for now.
