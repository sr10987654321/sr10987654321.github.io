Word problem

Find the equal groups.

There are 6 trays with 27 seedlings on each tray. How many seedlings are there altogether?

Equation + working + label

6 × 27 = (6 × 20) + (6 × 7) = 120 + 42 = 162 There are 162 seedlings.

Two-step problem

Answer the actual question.

There are 8 packs of 24 bookmarks. Then 35 bookmarks are sold. How many are left?

Do both steps

8 × 24 = 192 192 − 35 = 157 There are 157 bookmarks left.
First find the total. Then use the new information.
Explain an error

Use maths to prove it.

“32 × 6 = 182”

What should the answer be?

Explain

30 × 6 = 180 2 × 6 = 12 180 + 12 = 192 The correct answer is 192.

Open challenge

Make a plan. Try it. Improve it.

Make 12 prize bags for no more than $120. Pencils cost $2. Badges cost $3. What could go in each bag?

One possible plan

3 pencils + 1 badge = $6 + $3 = $9 per bag 12 × $9 = $108 $12 is left.
