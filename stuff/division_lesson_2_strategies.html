STRATEGY 1 · THINK MULTIPLICATION
TURN IT AROUND.
36 ÷ 6 = ?
Ask: 6 times WHAT equals 36?
6 × 6 = 36, so 36 ÷ 6 = 6.
Division and multiplication are connected.
