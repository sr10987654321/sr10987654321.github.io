IMITATE · Talk for Writing

First, borrow the pattern

The figure stood beneath the streetlight. His face was hidden.

Read both ideas aloud. What word could connect them?

The figure stood beneath the streetlight, but his face was hidden.

NOTICE · Talk for Writing

Why did but work?

The figure stood beneath the streetlight, but his face was hidden.

But signals a contrast: the figure is visible, yet important information is not.

How does this make the reader curious?

SORT · Talk for Writing

Three conjunction families

Coordinating

and · but · or · so

Connect ideas of equal importance

Subordinating

because · although · when · while · until · as

Add time, reason or contrast

Correlative

either…or · both…and · not only…but also

Connect linked choices or ideas

MODEL · Talk for Writing

I do: add a second clue

Two ideas:

The figure did not move. His shadow stretched across the pavement.

Although the figure did not move, his shadow stretched across the pavement.

We keep the figure mysterious; the conjunction makes the contrast more unsettling.

TOGETHER · Talk for Writing

We do: build a sentence

Use when, while, although, because or but to add a clue.

EXAMPLE · Talk for Writing

A possible paragraph

The street was deserted, but a tall figure stood beneath the flickering streetlight. Although his face was hidden by a dark hat, something about him seemed strangely familiar. The girl froze when the stranger took a step towards her. He lifted his head as the wind fell silent.

Spot the conjunctions. Which sentence is the most suspenseful, and why?
