How to check if a website is any good
  • Who wrote it? NASA runs the telescope, so NASA websites know best.
  • When was it written? Roman launched on 30 August 2026. Older pages say it "will launch" — still useful, but check the date.
  • Can you find it twice? If two websites say the same thing, you can trust it more.
  • Is it a fact or a guess? "It launched at 7:26 in the morning" is a fact. "It will change everything" is somebody's opinion.
