Works directly with the school system's class list export: Full Name, Form Group (Current), Gender, House (Current), Year Level (Current) — column order doesn't matter, they're matched by name. Preferred names in brackets are used automatically, room numbers are pulled out of the form group, and gender splits each year into Boys / Girls / Other. A manual format also works: First Name, Last Name, Year, House, Room, Gender.

Name handling: Jonathan (Jack) Smith → Jack Smith · Sarah Jane Wilson → Sarah Jane-Wilson
Form group: Year 4 Room 7 → room 7
Gender values: M / Male / Boy → Boys · F / Female / Girl → Girls · anything else (NB, X, Other…) → Other.
Year values: K, PP, 1–6 (with or without the word "Year").
